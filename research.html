Research & Projects

Research & projects

My research sits where brand management meets marketing strategy, digital consumer behaviour and business ethics. Most of it is quantitative, from brand-level panel data to survey-based structural models, and it increasingly uses computational text analysis.

Themes

  • Brand equity and brand value

    How brand equity is conceptualised and measured, from brand-level scanner data in fast-moving consumer goods to the digital environment.

    Towards a unified theory of brand equity (2015); Digital brand equity (2025)

  • Marketing resources and performance

    How marketing investments, R&D, product innovation and differentiation translate into pricing power, market share and competitive advantage.

    Marketing resources, performance, and competitive advantage (2016)

  • Digital consumers and engagement

    User-generated content, electronic word of mouth, live-stream commerce and AI-based conversational agents, and how they shape consumer engagement.

    Non-sponsored brand-related user-generated content (2022)

  • Business ethics and sustainability

    The ethical side of international business, deliberate counterfeit consumption and the circular economy in small and medium-sized enterprises.

    The dark side of business practices (2025); Circular economy in SMEs (2026)

  • Research methods and open tools

    Critical use of structural equation modelling, reproducibility in management research, and open-source Stata modules for theory-aware literature reviews.

    The use and misuse of SEM (2014); Stata modules (2026)

Current projects

  • Digital brand equity
    Conceptualising and measuring brand equity in digital markets, with Stephen France and Bianca Kazandjian (Journal of Business Research, 2025), and a systematic literature review with lexicographic analysis, with Romain Sohier (AMS World Marketing Congress, 2024).
  • AI and consumer experience
    How technology-based autonomous agents, such as virtual voice assistants and AI chatbots, affect consumer experience, using natural language processing; with Mustafeed Zaman and K. Mohamed Jasim.
  • Destination branding
    Tourist expenditure and consumption in cities as brand destinations; with Mustafeed Zaman and Piyush Sharma.
  • Competitive dynamics
    How consumer demand shapes inter-firm competitive dynamics, and the role of competitive intelligence; with Richard Priem, Iiro Vaniala and Wei Hou.
  • Literature-review software
    Stata modules for theory-aware literature review, construct discovery and citation-network analysis. See Software.
