The role and importance of competitive intelligence in competitive dynamics: Two exploratory surveys

Nebojša Davčik (ISCTE – University Institute of Lisbon) and Richard Priem (Neeley School of Business, Texas Christian University)

The paper reports two exploratory studies prepared for a broader project on competitive intelligence (CI) in the strategic management subfield of competitive dynamics. The first is a survey of members of Strategic and Competitive Intelligence Professionals (SCIP) about how their firms use CI. The second consists of structured interviews with managers on their use of CI in the Italian fast-moving consumer goods sector. The discussion identifies three challenges for contemporary practice: the managerial shift of CI, the importance of knowledge and data availability for SMEs, and the role of social media.

Design
Study 1: online survey of SCIP members, 58 responses, November 2017 to January 2018. Study 2: semi-structured interviews with five managers in the Italian FMCG industry.
Keywords
Competitive intelligence; competitive dynamics; strategic management; FMCG; SMEs; social media
