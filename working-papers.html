Diagnosing and theorising with topic models: An inductive–deductive workflow for systematic literature reviews with litdiscover in Stata

Nebojša Davčik (EM Normandie Business School, Oxford)

The paper presents litdiscover, a Stata package that supports the analytical development of a systematic literature review, from a screened text corpus to synthesis and interpretation. The command fits a latent Dirichlet allocation topic model to abstracts through Stata’s Python integration and reports three diagnostics: topic coherence, topic stability across random initialisations, and term rankings that combine frequency with exclusivity. It then relates topic shares to researcher-coded fields such as theory, context, method, and antecedent and outcome variables, producing topic-by-field tabulations, TCCM cross-tabulations and construct co-occurrence networks. The paper argues for keeping topic discovery and construct coding as separate analytical steps, and closes with guidance on interpreting the diagnostics jointly and on the approach’s limitations.

Design
Methods paper; a seven-stage workflow illustrated with a synthetic corpus of 500 abstracts.
Keywords
Systematic literature review; topic modelling; Stata; Python

The role and importance of competitive intelligence in competitive dynamics: Two exploratory surveys

Nebojša Davčik (ISCTE – University Institute of Lisbon) and Richard Priem (Neeley School of Business, Texas Christian University)

The paper reports two exploratory studies prepared for a broader project on competitive intelligence (CI) in the strategic management subfield of competitive dynamics. The first surveys members of Strategic and Competitive Intelligence Professionals (SCIP) about how their firms use CI. The second consists of structured interviews with managers on their use of CI in the Italian fast-moving consumer goods sector. The discussion identifies three challenges for contemporary practice: the managerial shift of CI, the importance of knowledge and data availability for SMEs, and the role of social media.

Design
Study 1: online survey of SCIP members, 58 responses, November 2017 to January 2018. Study 2: semi-structured interviews with five managers in the Italian FMCG industry.
Keywords
Competitive intelligence; competitive dynamics; strategic management; FMCG; SMEs; social media
