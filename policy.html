About these data

A policy citation records that a policy document cites a piece of research. It shows that the work is read and used as evidence beyond academia. It does not show, on its own, that the research shaped a particular decision. Overton counts working papers from economic research institutes and some academic outlets as policy sources, alongside reports from governments and intergovernmental bodies.

Titles, issuers and page references are as recorded by Sage Policy Profiles; titles in other languages are followed by an English translation.
