Marketing · Branding · Business ethics

I study how brands and marketing resources create value for firms, consumers and society, and I build open-source tools that make literature reviews more transparent and reproducible.

I am an Associate Professor of Marketing at EM Normandie Business School, Oxford campus, and Associate Editor of Business Ethics, the Environment & Responsibility. I serve on the editorial boards of the Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science, the Journal of Business Research, the Journal of Product & Brand Management and the Italian Journal of Marketing.

Research themes

  • Brand equity and brand value

    How brand equity is conceptualised and measured, from brand-level scanner data in fast-moving consumer goods to the digital environment.

    Towards a unified theory of brand equity (2015); Digital brand equity (2025)

  • Marketing resources and performance

    How marketing investments, R&D, product innovation and differentiation translate into pricing power, market share and competitive advantage.

    Marketing resources, performance, and competitive advantage (2016)

  • Digital consumers and engagement

    User-generated content, electronic word of mouth, live-stream commerce and AI-based conversational agents, and how they shape consumer engagement.

    Non-sponsored brand-related user-generated content (2022)

  • Business ethics and sustainability

    The ethical side of international business, deliberate counterfeit consumption and the circular economy in small and medium-sized enterprises.

    The dark side of business practices (2025); Circular economy in SMEs (2026)

  • Research methods and open tools

    Critical use of structural equation modelling, reproducibility in management research, and open-source Stata modules for theory-aware literature reviews.

    The use and misuse of SEM (2014); Stata modules (2026)

Short biography

Nebojša S. Davčik is an Associate Professor of Marketing at EM Normandie Business School, Oxford campus. His research examines brand equity, marketing resources and performance, digital consumer engagement and business ethics, and has appeared in the Journal of Business Research, the European Journal of Marketing, the Asia Pacific Journal of Management, Business Strategy and the Environment and the Journal of Product & Brand Management, among others. He is Associate Editor of Business Ethics, the Environment & Responsibility and serves on the editorial boards of the Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science, the Journal of Business Research, the Journal of Product & Brand Management and the Italian Journal of Marketing. He holds a PhD in Economics and Management from the University of Padua and was previously Assistant Professor of Marketing at ISCTE – University Institute of Lisbon. He also develops open-source Stata software for literature review and text analysis.
