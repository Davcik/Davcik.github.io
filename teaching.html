Teaching

Teaching

I teach digital marketing, branding, e-commerce management, and digital assets at undergraduate, master’s and doctoral levels. I have taught in the United Kingdom, Portugal, Italy, Sweden and Serbia.

Approach

My courses connect marketing theory with the analytical and ethical questions raised by digital markets. Students work with real brands, platforms and data, and are asked to justify their recommendations with evidence as well as creativity.

At doctoral level, I teach brand management, research methods and research publishing, with an emphasis on transparent and reproducible empirical work.
